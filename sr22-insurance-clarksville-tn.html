What is an SR-22?

An SR-22 is not insurance itself — it is a certificate your insurer files with the state to prove you carry the minimum liability coverage Tennessee requires. If the state asks for an SR-22, you need an active auto policy and a timely filing to keep or restore your driving privileges.

When Tennessee requires an SR-22

Common reasons drivers in Clarksville need an SR-22 include:

  • DUI or DWI conviction
  • Reckless driving or serious moving violations
  • License suspension or reinstatement after a lapse
  • At-fault accident while uninsured

How long you will need it

In Tennessee, SR-22 requirements typically last three to five years, depending on your situation. You must keep continuous coverage the entire time. A lapse can reset the clock and extend the requirement.
