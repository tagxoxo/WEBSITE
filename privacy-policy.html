Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Millennium Insurance (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our Website, submit a quote request, contact our office, or participate in our text messaging program. This policy applies to visitors and customers in Tennessee and elsewhere in the United States.

1. Who We Are

Millennium Insurance is an independent insurance agency located at 313 Dover Rd, Clarksville, TN 37042. We help individuals and businesses obtain insurance quotes and coverage through licensed agents and third-party insurance carriers. We are not an insurance company; we do not issue insurance policies.

Contact:
Phone: (931) 647-2434
Mail: 313 Dover Rd, Clarksville, TN 37042

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information: name, phone number, mailing address, and ZIP code.
  • Quote and insurance information: coverage types requested, vehicles, property details, business information, and other details you choose to provide in forms or conversations with our agents.
  • Communications: records of phone calls, text messages, and in-person visits when you contact us or we contact you about insurance services.
  • Website usage information: browser type, device type, IP address, pages viewed, referring URLs, and general usage data collected through standard server logs or similar technologies.
  • Language preference: if you use our English/Spanish language toggle, your preference may be stored locally in your browser.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through our Website forms (such as Social Security numbers, driver’s license numbers, payment card numbers, health information, or detailed claims history). Our agents will collect necessary information through secure channels when required.

3. How We Collect Information

  • When you submit a quote request or contact form on our Website.
  • When you call, text, or visit our office.
  • When you opt in to receive text messages from us.
  • When you interact with our licensed agents for quotes, policy service, or claims assistance.
  • Automatically when you browse our Website (see Section 8).

4. How We Use Your Information

We use information we collect to:

  • Respond to quote requests and provide insurance information.
  • Communicate with you about coverage options, policy service, renewals, and account-related matters.
  • Send text messages and other communications you have agreed to receive.
  • Submit information to insurance carriers and underwriting partners to obtain quotes or bind coverage, when applicable.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our Website and customer service.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, and licensing obligations.
  • Protect against fraud, unauthorized activity, and legal claims.

5. SMS / Text Messaging Program

If you provide your mobile phone number and opt in (including by submitting a form on our Website where SMS consent language is displayed), you agree to receive text messages from Millennium Insurance. These messages may include both non-marketing messages (such as responses to quote requests, appointment reminders, and policy-related updates) and marketing messages (such as information about insurance products and services), including messages sent using automated technology.

  • Message frequency varies based on your interactions with us.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier.
  • Consent is not a condition of purchase. You do not have to agree to receive text messages to obtain a quote or buy insurance.
  • To opt out at any time, reply STOP to any message. You may receive a final confirmation text.
  • For help, reply HELP or contact us at (931) 647-2434.
  • We do not share your opt-in consent or phone number with third parties for their own marketing purposes.
  • Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

We maintain records of SMS consent, opt-outs, and message logs as required for compliance with applicable telecommunications laws, including the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and related FCC rules, where applicable.

6. How We Share Information

We may share information with:

  • Insurance carriers and brokers to obtain quotes, underwrite policies, and service your account.
  • Service providers that help us operate our business, such as form processing providers (including Formspree), phone/SMS platforms, website hosting providers, and IT support vendors. These providers may only use information as needed to perform services for us.
  • Professional advisors such as attorneys, accountants, or auditors when necessary.
  • Legal and safety purposes when required by law, court order, subpoena, or government request, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect rights, safety, or property.
  • Business transfers in connection with a merger, sale, or transfer of business assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

We do not sell your personal information for money.

7. Insurance-Specific Notice

When you request a quote or apply for coverage, information you provide may be shared with one or more insurance companies to rate, underwrite, and issue a policy. Those carriers have their own privacy practices. Once a policy is placed, the issuing carrier’s privacy policy may govern much of the information related to that policy.

Information on this Website is for general informational purposes only and is not legal, tax, or insurance advice.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our Website may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to remember preferences (such as language selection) and support basic site functionality. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain site features.

We do not currently use third-party advertising cookies on this Website. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, including servicing your request, maintaining business records, meeting legal and regulatory requirements, and resolving disputes. SMS opt-out records are retained to honor your preferences.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no website, text message, or phone communication is completely secure. Please use caution when sharing information online.

11. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Opt out of marketing text messages by replying STOP.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information we maintain.
  • Ask questions about how we use and share your information.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the information in Section 1. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights where prohibited by law.

12. California Residents

If you are a California resident, you may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), including the right to know, delete, and correct personal information, and to opt out of certain sharing. As described above, we do not sell personal information for money. To submit a request, contact us at the information in Section 1.

13. Other State Privacy Rights

Residents of certain states (including Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, and others) may have additional privacy rights under state law. Contact us to learn more or submit a request. We will respond as required by applicable law.

14. Third-Party Links

Our Website may contain links to third-party websites (such as insurance carriers or partner sites). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing information.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be posted on this page. Your continued use of the Website or our services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy, to the extent permitted by law.
