Who needs non-standard auto insurance?

Non-standard auto is for drivers who do not fit the clean-record profile most national carriers prefer. You may need it if you have:

  • Multiple tickets or moving violations
  • One or more at-fault accidents
  • A DUI or DWI on your record
  • A newly licensed or young driver in the household
  • A lapse in coverage — even a short one
  • A foreign or international driver's license
